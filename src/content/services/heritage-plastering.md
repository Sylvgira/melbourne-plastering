Expert period restoration and heritage plastering for Melbourne's character homes. Mark specialises in matching and restoring original cornices, ceiling roses, archways, and decorative mouldings — bringing period details back to life with traditional techniques.

### What we do

- Ornamental cornicing — new and restoration
- Ceiling roses, domes and panels
- Barrel vaulted ceilings
- Stucco finishes
- Lathe and plaster repairs and removal
- Matching existing period details
