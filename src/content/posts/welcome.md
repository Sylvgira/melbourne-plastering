Melbourne is full of beautiful period homes — Victorian, Edwardian, Federation — each with its own character and craft. When it comes to restoring or repairing the plasterwork in these homes, modern materials and quick fixes can ruin what makes them special.

That's where heritage-specific plastering skills matter. Matching an existing cornice profile, repairing a ceiling rose without losing the detail, or recreating a barrel vault requires an eye for proportion and a knowledge of traditional techniques.

### What makes heritage plastering different

- Cornice profiles vary by era — a Victorian cornice is different from an Edwardian one
- Lathe and plaster construction needs careful handling — modern plasterboard won't bond the same way
- Ornamental details like ceiling roses and archways are cast, not mass-produced
- Matching stucco finishes requires the right mix and application method

If you've got a period home in Melbourne and need plastering work, the best approach is to call someone who's done it before — ideally, for the last 30 years.
